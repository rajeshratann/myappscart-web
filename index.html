The idea behind MyAppsCart

MyAppsCart is the consumer app division of Kirosys Technologies Private Limited, and it started with a simple frustration: most mobile apps are cluttered, demand too much, and break the moment you go offline. We set out to build the opposite — lean, honest apps that respect your time, your storage, and your privacy.

The name comes from the idea of a curated cart: a small collection of hand-picked apps, each built with care, each with a clear purpose. No filler. We'd rather ship one app that does its job beautifully than ten that feel rushed.

Almost every app we release follows the same rules: works offline, needs no account, and has no hidden tracking. Where an app genuinely cannot — Shubhzo connects you to real vendors and real money, so it needs a server and a sign-in — the contract becomes collect only what the job needs, and never sell it. That much holds for everything in the cart.
