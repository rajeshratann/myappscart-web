The idea behind MyAppsCart

MyAppsCart started with a simple frustration: most mobile apps are cluttered, demand too much, and break the moment you go offline. We set out to build the opposite — lean, honest apps that respect your time, your storage, and your privacy.

The name comes from the idea of a curated cart: a small collection of hand-picked apps, each built with care, each with a clear purpose. No filler. We'd rather ship one app that does its job beautifully than ten that feel rushed.

Every app we release follows the same rules: works offline, needs no account, and has no hidden tracking. That's the contract — and we intend to keep it with every app in the cart.
