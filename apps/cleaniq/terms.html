🧭 Description of Service

Cleaniq AI is a privacy-first, on-device storage cleaner that helps you free up space. Features include:

  • AI detection of duplicate, similar, and blurry photos
  • Screenshot, large-file, and old-download cleanup
  • Junk, cache, empty-folder and leftover installer (.apk) cleaning
