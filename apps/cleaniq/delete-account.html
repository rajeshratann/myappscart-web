✅ Cleaniq AI has no account and no cloud storage. Everything the app stores lives on your device only — so deleting your data is as simple as clearing it in the app or uninstalling.

✅ Option 1 — Clear data from within the app

The quickest way to remove your Cleaniq AI data while keeping the app installed:

  1. 1

    Empty the Recycle Bin

    Open Recycle Bin and tap Empty to permanently remove everything you've deleted (this also reclaims the space).

  2. 2

    Clear the app's stored data

    Go to Android Settings → Apps → Cleaniq AI → Storage → Clear Data. This wipes the scan index, settings, and all app-stored data.

  3. 3

    Revoke permissions (optional)

    Under Apps → Cleaniq AI → Permissions, remove media and all-files access if you no longer want the app to read your storage.

📱 Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Cleaniq AI permanently deletes all app-stored data — the scan index, recycle bin, and settings. Your own photos and files are never affected by uninstalling, because the app only ever read them; it never made its own copies.

  1. 1

    Long-press the Cleaniq AI icon

    Tap Uninstall, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Cleaniq AI → Uninstall.

  2. 2

    Confirm

    Android deletes all local app data immediately. There is nothing stored on our servers to remove.

  3. 3

    Empty the recycle bin first (recommended)

    If you want the space back right away, empty the app's recycle bin before uninstalling — otherwise those files remain in your storage until Android reclaims them.

📱 Reset your advertising identifier

Ads are served by Google AdMob using your device's advertising ID. To reset or delete it:

  1. 1

    Open Android Ad settings

    Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

  2. 2

    Reset or delete the advertising ID

    Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID, and enable Opt out of Ads Personalization if you prefer non-personalized ads.
