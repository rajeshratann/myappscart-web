ChandaKhata — Delivery Policy

How and when credits, Premium and gift coupons are delivered. Nothing is shipped.

📅  Last updated: 30 October 2026  ·  Applies to all purchases for ChandaKhata.

📦 Digital delivery only

Everything sold for ChandaKhata is digital. Nothing is shipped and there is no physical delivery or shipping charge.

⚡ When you receive it

  • Messaging credits are added to your in-app balance as soon as Cashfree confirms the payment, normally within a minute.
  • Premium is switched on for your account at the same moment, and its end date is shown on the Premium screen.
  • Gift coupons bought on this website are shown on screen right after payment and emailed to the address you entered, normally within a few minutes.
  • You also receive a payment confirmation by email when an email address is on file.

🕑 If something does not arrive

Reopen the app with internet on, or reload the payment result page; it checks the payment with Cashfree again. If the purchase still has not arrived within 24 hours, email us with the order ID and we will deliver it or refund you in full (see the refund policy).
