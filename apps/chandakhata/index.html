चंदा से हिसाब तकFrom collection to final accounts

Every feature is built around the doorstep: a collector with a phone, a donor in a hurry, and a treasurer who has to answer for every rupee.

⚡

10 सेकंड में रसीदReceipt in under 10 seconds

Name, amount, cash or UPI, save. The receipt has its number and is on its way to the donor before they have closed the door.

📶

बिना इंटरनेट भीWorks offline

Each phone holds a block of receipt numbers issued in advance, so a receipt written with no signal is final — not a temporary slip. Everything syncs when the network returns. UPI payments recorded offline are marked for the treasurer to verify.

📲

दानदाता को ऐप नहीं चाहिएDonors need no app

Receipts go by WhatsApp share, an SMS link or email, and open in any browser. Nobody is asked to install anything or sign up.

🤝

वादा अलग, रकम अलगPledges kept separate

"I'll give ₹5,000 next week" is recorded as a vaada, not as money. Pledged and received are tracked separately, so the totals never overstate what is in hand.

🧾

खर्च और बिलExpenses with bill photos

Log an expense with a photo of the bill. The treasurer reviews it and swipes to approve — no loose papers in a carry bag.

💵

नकद हैंडओवरCash handover by QR

When a collector hands cash to the treasurer, a QR scan records who handed over how much — and it works offline too. Everyone knows whose hands the money is in.

🔍

पारदर्शिताTransparency QR & public accounts

Put a QR on the pandal and anyone can see collections and spending. Donor names appear only with consent; गुप्त दान (Gupt Daan) stays anonymous. Phone numbers and addresses are never shown.

📊

रिपोर्टReports in PDF & Excel

Collection, expense and collector-wise reports as PDF or Excel, with charts for the committee meeting and an audit report for Premium.

🎨

20 डिज़ाइन20 receipt & invitation designs

Receipts and invitations in designs suited to a puja, a festival, a langar or a fund drive — with your committee's name on them.

👥

Organizer, treasurer, collector and member — each sees and does only what their role allows.
