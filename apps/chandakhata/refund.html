ChandaKhata — Refund & Cancellation Policy

When you can get your money back for credits, Premium and gift coupons, and how.

📅  Last updated: 30 October 2026  ·  Applies to all payments made in the ChandaKhata app and on this website.

🧾 What you can buy

ChandaKhata is free to use. You pay only for optional digital items: messaging credits (₹30 for 100, ₹125 for 500, ₹440 for 2,000), Premium (₹199 a month or ₹1,499 a year), and Premium gift coupons of the same value. All prices include GST. Payments are processed by Cashfree; we never see your card, UPI or bank details.

↩️ Refunds

  • Charged but not received. If money left your account but the credits, Premium or coupon did not arrive, write to us. We confirm with Cashfree and either deliver the purchase or refund the full amount.
  • Charged twice. A duplicate payment for the same order is refunded in full.
  • Unredeemed gift coupons. A coupon code that has not been redeemed can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Once redeemed, it cannot be refunded.
  • Messaging credits are non-refundable once added. A credit spent on an SMS or WhatsApp message that fails to send is returned to your balance automatically.
  • Premium that has started is non-refundable for the period already paid.

❌ Cancellation

Premium does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: it simply ends on its expiry date unless you buy it again. You can stop using the app or delete your account at any time from Settings. A payment that you abandon on the Cashfree page is not charged.

⏱️ How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through Cashfree, usually within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

✉️ Ask for a refund

Email us from any address with the order ID (in your payment confirmation email or on the payment result page), the date, the amount and your mobile number. We reply within 2 working days.
