⚡ Four fixes that solve most problems

1 · "The app won't open after an update"

Restart the device first. Do not uninstall — that removes your local database. If a restart doesn't help, write to us before uninstalling anything.

2 · "My second device isn't syncing"

Check, in order: is the hub device's app actually open (sync runs in the foreground only); are both devices on the same Wi-Fi network; and is there anything waiting in More → Records needing review? A flagged conflict freezes that record until a person chooses.

3 · "The tax on my bill looks wrong"

The CGST+SGST versus IGST split comes from your shop's state compared with the bill's place of supply. Check More → Business profile → State, then the customer's saved state — a customer saved with the wrong state will keep producing wrong bills until their record is fixed.

4 · "My stock has gone negative"

Something was sold that the app didn't know you had — usually stock that arrived without a purchase being recorded. Record the purchase, or a stock adjustment with a reason, and the balance corrects itself.

✉️ Write to us

One address, and a real person reads it. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

support@myappscart.com

What to include so we can help on the first reply

  • Platform and version — Android, iPhone, Mac or Windows, and the app version from More → Help.
  • What you were doing — the screen you were on and the step that failed.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot if there is anything to see.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.
Please don't email us your backup file or your customer list unless we specifically ask, and never send us a PIN. We can almost always diagnose a problem from a description and a screenshot.

💡 Requesting a feature

Feature requests are welcome and genuinely read. The most useful ones describe the situation at your counter rather than the solution — "I sell loose sweets and customers ask for 250 g" tells us far more than "add a decimals option", because it lets us solve the real problem.

You can also send feedback from inside the app: More → Feedback.

🐞 Reporting something that looks wrong with a number

We take arithmetic bugs more seriously than anything else, because a wrong figure travels as far as a filed return. If a total, a tax split, a stock balance or a khata balance looks wrong:

  1. Note the document number and the exact figures shown.
  2. Take a screenshot of the bill or the statement.
  3. Email us with "Figure check" in the subject line — these go to the front of the queue.

🧾 About billing and plans

Subscription questions — a payment that succeeded but hasn't unlocked, a refund, or changing plan — go to the same address. Include the date and amount of the charge and the email or account you paid with.

If a payment succeeded but the plan hasn't appeared, first reopen More → Subscription and give it a moment: the app checks with the server rather than trusting itself, so it can take a few seconds.
