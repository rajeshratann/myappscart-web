The core decision

The bill does not wait for the network.

Most billing apps are a website in a wrapper: no signal, no sale. HisabNova writes every record to SQLite on your own device first and treats the cloud as a place to copy them to. A basement shop, a village market, a power cut, an expired data pack — none of them stop a bill being raised, printed and handed over.

Under the counter

How the numbers are made

GST is not one rate applied once — it is an ordered pipeline where the sequence changes the answer. These figures follow the same ₹9,947 bill from the screens above, so nothing here is illustrative arithmetic.

Figure 1 · The same bill, split two ways

Where the buyer is decides whether the tax is CGST + SGST or a single IGST. The taxable value and the total never change — only the split does.

Taxable value CGST SGST IGST

CGST and SGST are each half the rate, rounded independently and then held equal by construction, as the law requires. Nil-rated, exempt, non-GST and zero-rated lines carry no tax at all — they are not "0%" arithmetic, they are a different category.

Figure 2 · The order the invoice is computed in

Swap steps 2 and 3 and a shop with an invoice-level discount pays the wrong tax. The sequence below is the one GST requires.

That identity is asserted by the test suite on every computed invoice, so a bill whose parts do not reconcile cannot be saved in the first place.

Figure 3 · Stock is a sum, never a stored number

Basmati Rice, one month, in kilograms. Nothing writes a balance — the balance is what the signed movements add up to, so a crash or a sync mid-way cannot leave a counter half-updated.

Balance Stock in (+) Stock out (−)

Each movement carries a typed reason, and the sign has to match it — a "sale" that tried to add stock is rejected rather than recorded. Batch and warehouse balances are the same sum, narrowed.

First launch on a Mac. HisabNova is not yet notarised by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS will say it cannot verify the app. That is macOS not recognising us yet — it is not a problem with the download.

Older guides say to right-click and choose Open — Apple removed that shortcut in macOS 15, so on Sequoia and later it no longer works.

Worth checking the download is intact before you install it — paste this in Terminal and the number should match the one above:

Questions

The things shopkeepers ask first

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes — billing, stock, khata, reports, PDFs and printing all run in aeroplane mode. Records are written to a database on your own device and are complete the moment you tap Save. Syncing to another device or to the cloud is something that happens afterwards, if you have set it up.

Do I need a GST number to use it?

No. Billing, stock and khata all work without one. Add your GSTIN later in the business profile and it appears on documents from that point on — nothing already recorded is disturbed. The GSTIN is also what the app reads the state code from, and the state code is what decides CGST/SGST versus IGST.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your books stay on your device, readable and exportable. An expired subscription stops you adding to them; it never takes them away, and backup, restore, CSV and Excel export are not gated on any plan.

Can two people bill at the same time?

Yes. One device runs as the hub and the others pair with it over the shop's Wi-Fi. Each device bills on its own invoice series — INV/… on the hub, INV-B/…, INV-C/… on the others — which GST permits provided each series is consecutive and unique. That is what lets two tills bill offline at once without ever minting the same number.

What if two devices change the same thing?

Nothing merges itself. The record is flagged, both versions are shown side by side under More → Records needing review, and an owner or admin picks which survives. Every choice writes an audit entry naming who made it.

Is the money arithmetic exact?

Yes, and deliberately so. No floating-point number goes near a rupee — amounts are exact base-10 decimals stored as text. Division is the only operation that can lose precision, so it refuses to run without being told a scale and a rounding mode. Split amounts are allocated by largest-remainder so the parts sum back to the whole exactly.

Can I sell things by weight?

Yes. Quantity carries its own unit and decimals — 2.5 kg of rice, 1.75 m of wire, 250 g of sweets — through pricing, stock and the printed bill. For unlabelled loose stock the app can also mint a real in-store barcode so it scans at the till.

Will it file my GST return for me?

It prepares the return, not the filing. GSTR-1 gives you B2B/B2C splits, rate-wise taxable and tax, and an HSN summary, exportable as CSV; there is a GSTR-3B summary view; and e-invoice and e-way bill payloads are generated in the schema the portal expects. The upload itself still happens on the government portal.

Does the AI see all my data?

No. The assistant is only reachable when you open it, and it is handed a short live summary — sales, dues, health, top items — not the database. It is instructed never to invent your figures, only to reason from what it is given. If you never open it, nothing is ever sent.

Is this a backup?

Wi-Fi sync is not. Several devices holding copies is resilience, not retention, and GST records must survive six years. Use scheduled exports or cloud backup alongside it — the guide has a page on what to do weekly.

Can I run it on a shop computer?

Yes — macOS and Windows builds run the same books, with a sidebar instead of a bottom bar, keyboard shortcuts, USB barcode-gun support and one-press printing to the counter's printer. Bill scanning (OCR) is the one feature with no desktop implementation.
