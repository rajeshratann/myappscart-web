🧭 Description of Service

JiVault is an AI-powered document management app that provides:

  • Document scanning with auto-crop and enhancement
  • OCR (optical character recognition) in 12 languages
  • Secure local document vault with biometric protection
  • PDF tools: merge, split, annotate, convert
  • Cloud backup via Google Drive (optional, user-initiated)
  • Digital signatures, QR scanner, voice transcription, reminders, and notes
  • AI-powered image enhancement and text summarization via cloud functions
