🧭 Description of Service

Gully Cricket Scorer is an offline-first cricket scoring app for street and gully, society, college, corporate and box cricket. Features include:

  • Ball-by-ball scoring by a single scorer, with match presets and configurable house rules
  • Undo, redo and editing of past balls
  • Scorecard images and PDF scorecards for sharing, including to WhatsApp
  • Player and group statistics, leaderboards, MVP points and match charts
  • Tournaments with fixtures, points table, net run rate and scorer invites
  • Live score links that others can open in a web browser
  • Optional cloud backup and sync when you sign in

Scoring works on your device without an internet connection. Cloud backup, tournaments and live links need an internet connection and a signed-in account.
