Delete Your Account & Data

Simple, complete account removal for Gully Cricket Scorer.

✅ Never signed in? Then there is no account and nothing in our cloud — everything lives on your phone, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes it. The steps below are for people who signed in for backup, tournaments or live links.

✅ Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (instant)

  1. 1

    Open the app and stay signed in

    Open Gully Cricket Scorer and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete, with an internet connection.

  2. 2

    Go to Settings → Account → Delete account

    Read the summary and confirm twice. This cannot be undone.

  3. 3

    Choose what happens on this phone

    You'll be asked about the matches and players stored on the phone: Keep on phone to carry on scoring offline with them, or Delete from phone too to wipe them as well.

⚠️ What is deleted — and what is kept

Data Where stored On account deletion
Account & sign-in (name, phone number or email) Firebase (our cloud) Deleted
Synced matches, ball-by-ball events, players, teams, groups and rule presets Firebase (our cloud) Deleted
Tournaments you own Firebase (our cloud) Deleted (paid ones: your details removed — see below)
Live score links you created Firebase (our cloud) Deleted
Player photos & team logos you uploaded Firebase (our cloud) Deleted
Notification tokens Firebase (our cloud) Deleted
Matches, players & photos on your phone Your device (local) Kept unless you choose “Delete from phone too”
Backup files you saved or shared Wherever you put them Delete them yourself
Purchase records Google Play & RevenueCat Kept for tax, refunds & fraud prevention
Anonymous analytics & crash reports Firebase Expire automatically (up to 14 months)

Results you scored in someone else's tournament stay with that tournament, without your account details, so their points table still adds up. For a paid tournament you organised, we remove your personal data and the organiser link.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Gully Pro yearly subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

✉️ Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app or the phone, email us from the email address you signed in with, or include the phone number you signed in with:

Email: support@myappscart.com
Subject: Delete my account — Gully Cricket Scorer

We will verify the request, delete your account and cloud data within 30 days, and confirm by email.

✉️ Request via email

📱 Remove the data on your phone

To remove matches and players stored only on your phone — whether or not you ever signed in:

  1. 1

    Clear the app's storage

    Go to Android Settings → Apps → Gully Cricket Scorer → Storage → Clear storage.

  2. 2

    Or uninstall the app

    Long-press the app icon and tap Uninstall. Android deletes all of the app's local data.
