1Getting started

मोबाइल नंबर से लॉगिन करें और अपनी भूमिका चुनें।

  1. Choose your language. हिन्दी or English on the first screen. Change it later in More → Settings → Language.
  2. Log in with your mobile number. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent by SMS. There are no passwords. You can also continue with Google, then verify your mobile once.
  3. Tell us your name. Institutes and teachers see this name.
  4. Choose what you want to do:
    • Create a Coaching Institute — for owners.
    • Join an Institute as Teacher — enter the teacher code your institute shared (e.g. TEACH-82651).
    • Join a Batch as Student — enter the batch code or scan its QR.
    • Access as Parent — if the institute already has your number as a parent, your children appear automatically.

One number, many profiles

A teacher who is also a parent, a parent with two children, or a teacher in two institutes uses one login. Tap the institute name at the top of the Home screen to switch profile. Each profile keeps its own data.

2Setting up your institute

संस्थान का नाम, लोगो, मुहर और हस्ताक्षर — ये हर रसीद पर अपने आप छपते हैं।

Institute profile

Coaching name, owner name, mobile, email and address are required. Add your logo, stamp and signature (upload a photo or draw it on screen) — they are printed automatically on receipts, ID cards, report cards and certificates. A short code such as BFC is used in receipt numbers and student IDs. Add a GST number if you want GST tax invoices.

Edit later in More → Institute profile.

Institute settings

More → Institute settings holds the academic session, working days and hours, default fee due day, receipt prefix and GST invoice switch, joining approvals (auto / approval required / disabled — separately for teachers and students), attendance rules (auto-close, grace time, QR window, late after, low-attendance %), fee reminder schedule and parent alert options.

Setup checklist & sample data

Until you finish, the Home screen shows a checklist: profile → first batch → first student → first fee entry. Tap Try sample data to explore with a demo batch and five students, and Remove sample data to clear it in one tap.

Every new institute gets a free 14-day Professional trial, so you can try every feature before choosing a plan.

3Batches, timetable & holidays

बैच बनाएँ — समय, दिन और फ़ीस के साथ।

Create a batch

Batches → +. Required: name, start date, timing, working days, fee amount, fee frequency and due day. Optional: subject, class, description, cover photo, end date, maximum students (turns on the waitlist), teachers, UPI ID / QR for this batch, and joining permission.

  • Fee frequency: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly, one-time, or custom instalments.
  • Mid-session joining: charge a prorated fee, the full fee, or start from the next cycle.
  • Late fee: none, fixed, or per day after a grace period.

Inside a batch

Open a batch to see its sections: Overview · Students · Attendance · Fees · Notifications · Study Materials · Assignments · Tests & Results · Feedback · Discussion · Performance · Holidays · Reports · Settings. The Overview shows today's birthdays and holiday status. From the menu you can deactivate, complete or duplicate a batch for the next session.

Timetable

More → Timetable shows the week by batch or teacher. Cancel a class (it is marked Not held and never counts as an absence), reschedule it, or add an extra class. TutorlyPro warns you about holiday clashes and teachers booked in two places. Students get a reminder 30 minutes before class.

Holidays

More → Holidays → +. One date, several dates or a range, for one batch, several, or all. Students and parents are notified, the batch card shows the holiday colour and reason, and no attendance is taken that day.

4Adding students

कोड, QR, लिंक, खुद जोड़कर या Excel से — छात्र जोड़ने के पाँच तरीके।

1 · Share the batch code (fastest)

Open the batch → Invite code & QR. Share the code (e.g. MAT10-9274), QR or link on WhatsApp. Students or parents tap Join a Batch, enter the code and fill in the details. Depending on your setting they join at once, or appear under More → Join requests for you to approve.

You can set an expiry date and a maximum number of uses for each code, and revoke or regenerate it any time — students already in the batch stay.

2 · Add a student yourself

Students → +. Student name, mobile, class, date of birth, and parent name and mobile are needed. TutorlyPro warns you about possible duplicates (same mobile, or same parent + name). When that student or parent logs in with the number you entered, their profile links automatically — no code needed.

3 · Import from Excel

More → Import students. Pick a .xlsx or .csv file, check the column matching (English or Hindi headings work), fix any rows marked in red and import. Share the template file to see the expected columns.

4 · Enquiries and admission forms

More → Enquiries tracks leads: source, interest, follow-up date, demo class and status (New, Follow-up, Demo, Joined, Lost). Share your public enquiry or admission form link on Instagram or WhatsApp. Convert an enquiry into a student in one tap.

Waitlist, leaving and transfers

  • When a batch is full, new students join the waitlist in order. Admit them when a seat frees up.
  • Leaving: set an exit date. Future fees stop; anything already due stays collectable.
  • Transfer moves a student to another batch from a chosen date.

Students under 18

A minor's own login becomes fully active only after a parent gives consent in the app (More → Parent consent on the parent's phone). Until then the parent can already see everything.

Session rollover

At the end of the year: More → Session rollover. Choose the students to promote, map each old batch to its new batch, and confirm. Unpaid dues carry forward and last year's reports stay available.

5Teachers, staff & permissions

हर शिक्षक को केवल उसके बैच दिखते हैं।

More → Teachers & staff → Invite. Add by mobile number, or share a teacher invite code. Roles: Admin, Teacher, Accountant, Attendance Manager, Academic Coordinator, Receptionist and Branch Manager.

  • Batches: teachers see only the batches you assign them.
  • Permissions: every role starts with sensible defaults; switch individual permissions on or off (for example, let a teacher mark attendance but not change fees).
  • Activity log: More → Activity log shows who changed attendance, fees, results and students, and when.

6Attendance

सूची में सभी पहले से उपस्थित — सिर्फ़ अनुपस्थित पर टैप करें।

Mark by hand

Open the batch → Attendance → Mark. Everyone starts as Present; tap a student to change to Absent, Late or Leave. Approved leave is filled in for you. Save works even without internet.

QR attendance

  1. Teacher opens Today's QR for the batch. The code changes every 10 seconds, so a forwarded photo stops working.
  2. Students tap Scan QR on their Home screen and point the camera at it.
  3. Scans in the first minutes count as Present; later scans count as Late. One phone can mark one student — a sibling on the same phone needs the teacher's approval.

For classrooms without a screen, a printable static QR is available in the batch settings (less secure — you will see a warning).

Closing the day

Tap Close attendance, or let it close automatically after the class ends plus the grace time. Students not marked become Absent and parents get an absent alert. You can still correct it later; every change is logged.

No class today?

Holidays and classes marked Not held never create absences and don't count in the percentage.

Leave

Students or parents apply from More → Leave requests with dates, reason and an optional document. Teachers approve, reject or ask a question. Approved days show as Leave.

Reports

More → Attendance reports: daily, monthly and yearly, by batch or student, with late arrivals and the lowest-attendance list. Export to PDF or Excel.

Attendance % = (Present + Late) ÷ (Present + Late + Absent). Approved leave, holidays and not-held classes are left out.

7Fees, payments & receipts

फ़ीस अपने आप बनती है; भुगतान दर्ज करते ही रसीद तैयार।

Dues are created for you

For every active student, TutorlyPro creates each cycle's due from the batch fee — a few days before the due day, so reminders can go out on time. Mid-session joiners follow the batch rule, students on approved long leave can be paused, and students who leave stop getting new dues.

The month grid

Open a batch → Fees. Collected, Pending and Overdue cards sit on top; below, each student has a row of months: paid, pending, partly paid, waived or not yet due. Tap any cell to collect or adjust.

Record a payment

Fees → Collect fee or the student's Fees tab. Choose the amount and method (cash, UPI, bank transfer…). The payment goes to the oldest dues first; anything extra becomes credit for next time. Cash entries work offline — the receipt number arrives when the phone syncs.

UPI payments from parents

  1. The parent opens Pay, sees your UPI QR and the amount, and pays in any UPI app.
  2. They enter the UTR / transaction ID (and can attach a screenshot). The status becomes Payment claimed.
  3. You get a notification. Check your bank app, then Confirm (a receipt is created) or Reject with a reason in More → Payment claims.

A UTR that was already used in your institute is rejected automatically.

Receipts

Each receipt has your logo, details, a unique number per financial year (e.g. BFC/2026-27/0045), the student and batch, months covered, method and reference, previous outstanding, amount paid, balance, signature and stamp. Share it on WhatsApp, SMS (secure link), email, or as a PDF. Turn on GST invoices in Institute settings to show SAC code and CGST/SGST or IGST.

Discounts and corrections

From a due or payment: discount, scholarship, waiver, late fee, refund, cancel payment, credit, carry forward or instalment change. Each needs a reason and is logged. Cancelled receipt numbers are never reused. Set a sibling discount in More → Fee settings.

Reminders

Automatic reminders go to students and parents a few days before the due date, on the due date, and then every few days until paid (you set how many). They never go out between 9 PM and 7 AM. Use Remind all pending on the Fees tab for a manual nudge.

8Online payments with Cashfree

ऑनलाइन फ़ीस सीधे आपके अपने Cashfree खाते में जाती है।

On the Professional plan, parents can pay by UPI, card or net banking on a secure Cashfree page, and the money settles directly into your own Cashfree account. TutorlyPro never holds your fees.

  1. Open a Cashfree merchant account at merchant.cashfree.com and complete KYC.
  2. In Cashfree: Developers → API Keys. Copy your App ID and Secret Key.
  3. In TutorlyPro: More → Fee settings → Online payments (Cashfree). Paste both and save. Keys that start with TEST are test (sandbox) keys.

That's all for one-time payments: a parent taps Pay online, pays, and the receipt appears as soon as Cashfree confirms the payment.

AutoPay (recurring fees)

Parents can set up UPI AutoPay or e-NACH for a batch's recurring fee from their Fees screen. They approve the mandate in their own bank or UPI app — it never starts without their permission. For AutoPay, also add the webhook link shown in TutorlyPro to Cashfree → Developers → Webhooks.

Only the institute owner can connect or change the payment account. Keep your Secret Key private.

9Exams, results & report cards

अंक डालें — कुल, प्रतिशत, ग्रेड और रैंक अपने आप।

  1. More → Exams & results → +. Name, batch, subjects with maximum and passing marks, date, duration and instructions. Attach the question paper and answer key — the key stays hidden until you release it.
  2. Enter marks. Type a number and press Next to jump to the next student. Mark AB for absent. Bulk upload from Excel is available on Professional.
  3. Review. Total, percentage, grade (from your grade scale), pass/fail, rank, class average and change from the previous exam are calculated for you.
  4. Publish. Each student and parent sees only their own result and gets a notification. A PDF report card is ready to share.

Need to fix a published result? Use Correct result with a reason — it is logged and the family is told. Set your grade bands in More → Grade scale. Term report cards combine several exams with weightage plus attendance % and remarks.

A student marked AB in every subject is left out of rank and averages; AB in only some subjects counts as 0 for those subjects.

Certificates

More → Certificates: course completion, topper, perfect attendance or participation — filled in automatically with your signature and stamp, numbered, and with a QR that anyone can scan to verify it.

10Materials & assignments

नोट्स, PDF, वीडियो और होमवर्क — एक जगह।

Study materials

More → Study materials → +. Upload PDFs, Word or PowerPoint files, audio, video, or add YouTube and web links. Take several photos of notes and they become one PDF. Organise by subject and chapter, pin important items, and allow offline download if you want. Students see a New tag on fresh material.

Assignments

More → Assignments → +. Title, description, batch, due date, attachments, optional marks, and how students submit (photo, document or text). Students submit from their phone; you review, give marks and comments, or ask for a correction. Students who haven't submitted get a reminder the day before the due date.

Points and leaderboard

In a batch's Performance section, set point rules (homework, participation, discipline — negative points allowed), award points to many students at once, and give badges like Star Student or Most Improved. The leaderboard shows names and points; rank and badges appear only if you turn them on.

11Messages, feedback & birthdays

एक अभिभावक, एक बैच या सभी को — एक टैप में संदेश।

Announcements

More → Send announcement. Choose recipients (one student or parent, a batch, several batches, all students, all parents, teachers or staff), pick a template (holiday, class cancelled, timing change, fee reminder, exam, result, birthday…) or write your own, and send now or schedule it. See delivery and read status in Message history.

Push notifications and in-app messages are always included. SMS and WhatsApp Business messages are available on higher plans; on any plan you can tap Share to WhatsApp to send the message from your own WhatsApp.

Quick contact

Every student profile has Call, SMS and WhatsApp buttons for the student or parent, with a message already typed in.

Feedback

Students rate their batch (1–5 stars with a message); the batch setting decides if it is anonymous. Teachers write remarks about a student by category and choose whether the parent and student can see each remark.

Discussion

Each batch has a discussion board for questions, posts and replies with photos and documents. Teachers can pin, delete, mute a student or switch the board off.

Birthdays

Teachers get a morning reminder and a banner with the student's photo. Wishes can go out automatically, or send your own from More → Birthdays.

Quiet hours and preferences

Automatic messages are never sent between 9 PM and 7 AM — they wait until morning. Everyone can mute categories in Settings → Notification preferences (fee and attendance alerts to parents only if the institute allows).

12For parents

अभिभावकों के लिए — सभी बच्चे एक ही लॉगिन में।

  • Log in with the mobile number you gave the institute. Your children appear on their own. If not, ask the institute to check your number, or join with the batch code.
  • More than one child? Switch between them at the top of the Home screen.
  • Home shows attendance and recent absences, fee status with a Pay button, upcoming tests, results, homework and teacher remarks.
  • Pay fees by UPI (enter the transaction ID afterwards), online through the institute's Cashfree page, or in cash. Download every receipt from the Fees tab.
  • Apply for leave from More → Leave requests.
  • Give consent for a child under 18 from More → Parent consent, so your child can use their own login.

13For students

छात्रों के लिए — समय सारणी, QR हाज़िरी, नोट्स और परिणाम।

  • Join with the batch code or QR your teacher shares.
  • Home shows today's classes, your attendance %, fees due, tests, latest results, new material and your points.
  • Scan QR in class to mark your attendance.
  • Open Study materials and Assignments, submit homework as photos, documents or text.
  • Your ID card (More → ID card) works offline and has a QR your teacher can scan.

14Reports, expenses & payouts

वसूली, बकाया, ख़र्च और मुनाफ़ा — एक नज़र में।

Dashboard and reports

The owner's Home shows students, teachers and batches, collection this month and year, pending and overdue fees, today's attendance, upcoming exams, birthdays, holidays, recent payments and anything waiting for you. More → Reports has institute, batch and student reports; tap the button on a student's profile for a one-page full student report. Export as PDF or Excel. Big reports are prepared in the background and you get a notification when they are ready.

Expenses and profit

More → Expenses: rent, electricity, salary, marketing, materials and more, with a bill photo. Mark monthly expenses as recurring. Profit = collections − expenses, by month (and by branch on Business).

Teacher payouts

More → Teacher payouts: pay each teacher a fixed monthly amount, per batch, per class held, or a % of the batch's collected fees. TutorlyPro calculates the month from classes held and fees collected; mark it paid and share the payout slip. Teachers see only their own payouts.

Branches

On Business, add branches in More → Branches, assign batches and staff, and filter dashboards and reports by branch.

16Privacy, safety & your account

  • Each institute's data is separate. Teachers see only their batches; families see only their own children.
  • No ads and no tracking on student profiles. Minors need parent consent.
  • App lock: Settings → App lock asks for your fingerprint or phone PIN — recommended for owners and accountants.
  • Changed your mobile number? Settings → My account → Change. You'll get OTPs on both numbers; if the old number is lost, the institute owner approves the change.
  • Export or delete your data from Settings. See the privacy policy and account deletion.

17Troubleshooting

I didn't get the OTP

Wait 30 seconds and tap Resend. You can request up to 5 OTPs an hour. Check that the number is an Indian mobile and that DND isn't blocking messages.

My child doesn't appear

The institute must have your exact mobile number as the parent number. Ask them to check it, then tap Check for my profiles on the welcome screen — or join with the batch code.

“Not synced” badge

Some entries are saved on the phone and waiting for internet. Keep the app open on a working connection and the badge disappears.

QR scan says “expired” or “too late”

The code changes every 10 seconds — scan the live screen, not a photo. Scans are accepted only during the class's scan window; ask the teacher to mark you manually if you arrived late.

I paid online but see no receipt

Receipts appear as soon as Cashfree confirms the payment, usually within seconds. Reopen the payment page or the Fees tab after a minute. If money was debited and nothing appears after an hour, contact your institute with the bank reference.
