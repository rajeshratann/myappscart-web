TutorlyPro — Privacy Policy

TutorlyPro helps tuition teachers and coaching institutes manage batches, attendance, fees and academics. Each institute is the data fiduciary for its students' and parents' data; TutorlyPro processes it on their behalf.

What we collect

Mobile number (login), name, optional email and photo; for students: details entered by the institute or the family (class, date of birth, parent contact), attendance, fee, result and assignment records.

Children

For students under 18, the profile becomes fully active only after a parent gives consent in the app (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). We do not show ads, profile or track children, and do not use their data for marketing.

Payments

Fees are paid directly to the institute (its own UPI or payment-gateway account). TutorlyPro never holds tuition fee money.

Messages

SMS use DLT-registered templates; automated messages are not sent between 9 PM and 7 AM. You can mute categories in the app (some fee/attendance alerts may be required by your institute).

Your rights

Export your data or delete your account from Settings in the app. Institute financial records (receipts) are retained as required by law.

Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase (India region for processing), isolated per institute and protected by access rules and audit logs.
