Privacy Policy

How TutorlyPro handles the data of institutes, teachers, students and parents.

Who we are

TutorlyPro is an app by MyAppsCart (India) that helps tuition teachers and coaching institutes manage batches, attendance, fees and academics. Each institute decides what data it records about its students and is responsible for it; we process that data on the institute's behalf to provide the service.

What we collect

  • Account: mobile number (for OTP login), name, and optionally email and photo.
  • Institute data: institute details, logo, stamp and signature; staff names, phone numbers and roles.
  • Student data entered by the institute or family: name, class, date of birth, parent name and contact, photo, batches, attendance, fees and receipts, marks and report cards, homework submissions, feedback, leave requests and points.
  • Device data: a push-notification token and a device identifier (used for one-phone-one-student QR attendance and security).

Children (students under 18)

A minor's own login becomes fully active only after a parent gives consent in the app, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Parents can withdraw consent at any time. We show no ads, do no behavioural tracking or profiling of students, and never use student data for marketing.

How data is used

Only to run the service: showing each person the information meant for them, sending notifications the institute or user has chosen, generating receipts and reports, and keeping the service secure (audit logs, fraud checks such as duplicate UPI references).

Who can see what

  • Each institute's data is isolated from other institutes.
  • Staff see what their role and assigned batches allow; teachers see only their batches.
  • Students and parents see only their own (or their children's) records and the batches they belong to.

Payments

Tuition fees are paid directly to the institute (cash, the institute's UPI, or the institute's own Cashfree account). Subscription payments for TutorlyPro are processed by Cashfree. We never see or store card numbers or UPI PINs.

Messages

SMS use DLT-registered templates; emails are sent from noreply@myappscart.com through Resend; WhatsApp messages use approved templates. Automated messages are not sent between 9 PM and 7 AM. You can mute categories in the app (some fee and attendance alerts to parents stay on if the institute requires them).

Where data is stored

On Google Firebase / Google Cloud, with processing in the Mumbai (asia-south1) region, encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by access rules and audit logs.

Retention

Data is kept while the account or institute uses the service. Deleted items stay in a 30-day recycle bin. Financial records (receipts, invoices) are kept as required by Indian tax law.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data. Use Settings → Export my data and Settings → Delete account in the app, or see account deletion. For records an institute holds about a student, contact the institute.

Contact

Grievance and privacy contact: support@myappscart.com.
