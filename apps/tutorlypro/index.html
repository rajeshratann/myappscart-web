अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवालQuestions

Do students and parents need to pay or sign up separately?

No. They log in with their mobile number for free. If you have already added them, their profile appears automatically when they log in.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Attendance, cash fee entries and drafts save on the phone and sync when the internet returns. A small “not synced” badge shows anything still waiting.

How do parents pay fees?

Three ways: scan your UPI QR and enter the transaction ID (you confirm it), pay online through your own Cashfree account (Professional plan), or pay cash at the institute. Every payment gets a receipt.

Is there a limit on students?

No. Students are unlimited on every plan, including Free. Plans differ only by batches, staff and premium features.

Can my teachers see fees or other batches?

Only if you allow it. Every staff role — teacher, accountant, receptionist, branch manager — has permissions you can switch on or off, and teachers see only the batches you assign.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Batches or staff above the new plan's limit become read-only until you upgrade again.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings → Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account.
