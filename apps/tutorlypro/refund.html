Refund & Cancellation Policy

For TutorlyPro subscriptions bought by institutes.

Tuition fees are not covered here. Fees you pay to a tuition or coaching institute go directly to that institute. For any refund of tuition fees, contact your institute — TutorlyPro does not receive or hold that money.

Free trial

Every new institute gets a 14-day Professional trial. No payment is taken; at the end you move to the Free plan unless you choose to buy.

Cancellation

You can turn off renewal any time in More → Subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and then moves to Free. Your data is kept.

Refunds

  • Monthly plans: a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first-ever paid month. Renewals are not refunded but you can cancel before the next renewal.
  • Yearly plans: a full refund within 7 days of purchase; after that, a pro-rated refund for the unused full months, if requested within 30 days of purchase.
  • Payment taken twice, or charged but the plan was not activated: always refunded in full.

How to request

Email support@myappscart.com from the institute owner's email with the institute name and invoice number (shown in the app's subscription payment history). Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Cashfree within 5–7 working days.
