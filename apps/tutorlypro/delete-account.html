Delete your account

How to delete your TutorlyPro account and what happens to your data.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TutorlyPro and go to More → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your login, profile, profile switcher entries, notifications and personal settings are deleted immediately. You are removed from any institute's staff list.

Without the app

Email support@myappscart.com from any address with the subject “Account deletion” and the mobile number you used. We confirm by OTP and delete within 7 days.

What is kept, and why

  • Institute owners must first close or hand over their institute, so students and parents are not cut off from their records.
  • Records an institute holds about a student (attendance, results, fee receipts) belong to that institute. Ask the institute to delete them; they are removed from the institute's records via its recycle bin within 30 days.
  • Financial records such as receipts and GST invoices are retained for 8 years as required by Indian tax law, then deleted.

Export your data first

More → Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of your profile and linked student records.
