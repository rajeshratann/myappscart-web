Quick answers

I didn't get the OTP

Wait 30 seconds and tap Resend. Up to 5 OTPs can be requested in an hour.

My child or batch doesn't appear

Ask the institute to check the mobile number they saved for you, then tap “Check for my profiles”, or join with the batch code.

I paid but there is no receipt

UPI payments entered with a transaction ID wait for the institute to confirm. Online (Cashfree) payments show a receipt as soon as Cashfree confirms, usually within seconds. If money was debited and nothing appears within an hour, share the bank reference with your institute.
