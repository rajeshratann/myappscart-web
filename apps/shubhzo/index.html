What Shubhzo is

One app for the family. One for the vendor.

Install it once. The first screen asks what brings you here — Plan an event or Grow my business — and you get an entirely different product, down to the colours. Sign in with a vendor account and you land on the Partner side no matter what you picked.

The side you get is a runtime choice

Most marketplaces ship two apps and ask you to find the right one. Shubhzo ships one binary that contains both. The theme, the navigation and the whole route table follow whichever side you are on — maroon and gold for the customer, indigo and teal for the partner — so a decorator who also plans her own daughter's wedding never has to wonder which app she is looking at.

Switching later lives in Settings → Switch app. It signs you out, because a session belongs to an account with one role.

Before any of that, the app asks which language to speak. Eleven are on the list, each in its own script, and the choice applies instantly — no restart, no re-download.

How a booking actually works

A small refundable token holds your date. The vendor verifies it in person by scanning your QR. Only when they accept do the two of you get each other's phone numbers.

The partner app

The other half of the marketplace

Vendors get a real business app, not a lead inbox: a pipeline, a calendar that cannot be double-sold, earnings arithmetic you can check, and insights that end in something to do.

📋

A pipeline in five stages

New, upcoming, awaiting confirmation, completed, archive. Every card carries its single next action, so there is never a job you have to work out what to do with.

📷

QR verification, six checks

The scanner runs all six of the platform's checks and reports each one separately, so a failure tells you exactly which one failed — and nothing about a customer who isn't yours.

📅

A calendar that can't double-sell

Accepting a job writes the date into your booked set, and the availability filter, the customer's date picker and the accept endpoint all read from it. Booked dates cannot be unblocked by hand.

💰

Earnings you can audit

Gross, commission, TDS, net. Settlement batches with UTRs, an account ledger, GST invoices you can share with your accountant, and a payout account gated behind KYC.

📈

Insights that end in an action

Where you rank on the searches you appear in, your twelve-month demand curve, the areas your customers come from, and how you compare with vendors like you — each finding links to the screen that fixes it.

🧾

Quote builder

Pre-filled from what the customer actually picked, with reusable templates, a discount and tax line, and a live preview of what you will be paid after commission.

🏪

Storefront, packages, portfolio

Your public profile with a customer's-eye preview, three package tiers with inclusions and exclusions, and a portfolio with a progress strip against the eight-photo mark.

🎁

Offers and plans

Percentage, flat or free add-on offers with audience, validity and redemption caps. Three plans where commission, search weight and lead credits move together.

👷

Team, documents, disputes

Crew with roles and per-job assignment, licences and insurance with expiry warnings that surface on Home, and disputes raised on a thread instead of over the phone.

Privacy is a server rule, not a UI trick

Nobody gets your number for asking.

On most marketplaces, an enquiry is a phone number handed to a stranger. Shubhzo does not send either party's phone or email to the other until the booking is confirmed — and it is the server that withholds them, returning masked values and a locked flag, so there is nothing for a modified app to reveal.

What the vendor's scanner checks

The vendor opens the scanner standing in front of you. Six things have to be true before the Accept button appears, and each is reported on its own line — so a failure says which one, not just "invalid".

✓Valid tokenThe code exists and was issued by the platform.
✓Valid bookingIt points at a real booking, not a stale one.
✓Valid providerIt was issued to this vendor, not a competitor.
✓Payment doneThe token was actually paid and verified.
✓Not expiredThe 24-hour window is still open.
✓Belongs to this customerThe person holding the phone is the one who booked.
The interesting case is the failure. Scan a token that belongs to another vendor and the "valid provider" check fails — and the other five checks stop naming anyone. No booking code, no customer name. A token number is guessable, so a failed scan tells you the token is not yours and nothing whatsoever about whose it is.
