Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 26, 2026

Overview

ChessVerse ("the App") is published by MyAppsCart, a brand of Kirosys Technology. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it. By using the App, you agree to the terms described here. It applies to the App on Android and on iPhone and iPad.

Every offline mode — playing the computer, puzzles, the story campaign, survival and Pass & Play on one device — works without an account. If you choose to sign in, you can also add friends, chat with them, play online against other people and appear on the global leaderboard.

Play as Guest — no account, progress stays on your device

If you choose Guest at the sign-in screen, the App never creates an account and never uploads your progress or any personal data to us. Progress (coins, gems, unlocked levels, puzzle history, game history, settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App. Ads are still shown, as described below.

Chat is limited by design. With players who are not your friends — for example an online opponent — you can only send a fixed set of preset phrases. Typed messages (up to 200 characters) are only possible between two players who have both accepted a friend request, and you can remove or block a friend at any time.

We never see your Apple or Google password — sign-in is handled entirely by Apple's, Google's or Play Games' own sign-in flow.

Automatically collected

  • Advertising identifiers — Google AdMob may collect an advertising ID and device/usage data to select and measure ads. Because ChessVerse may be played by children as well as adults, every ad request is marked child-directed and only non-personalized, general-audience ads are served — to every player, regardless of age. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and on iPhone and iPad it never asks for permission to.
  • Purchases — if you buy the optional "Remove Ads" subscription or a gem pack, Google Play or the App Store sends us a record of the purchase. We verify it on our server and keep it, linked to your account, so your purchase is honoured and can't be credited twice. We never see your payment details.

Kept only on your device

  • Your game history (including games you export as PGN)
  • Daily reminder notifications, which are scheduled on your device and never sent to us
  • Pass & Play games and the player names you type for them

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment or card details — Google Play and the App Store handle all payments directly
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, or file access

Redeeming a Coupon Code

On Android, Settings → Redeem Coupon Code links a single-use code to your signed-in account ID so it can't be reused by someone else. The code text itself is not personal data, and redemption requires you to be signed in first (Guests are prompted to sign in). Coupon codes are not available in the iPhone and iPad version.

  • To create and maintain your account, if you choose to sign in
  • To back up and restore your coins, gems, unlocks, and progress across devices
  • To display your name/photo on the public leaderboard (you can change your display name anytime)

Children's Privacy

ChessVerse is a mixed-audience app that may be played by both children and adults. Players who are not friends can only exchange preset phrases; typed messages are only possible between players who have both accepted a friend request, and any friend can be removed or blocked at any time. As described above, every ad shown in the App is treated as child-directed and non-personalized, for every player, regardless of age, since the App has no age-verification step.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has an account with personal data (from Apple, Google or Play Games sign-in) that you'd like removed, you can delete it in the App as described below, or contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Your Choices

  • Play as Guest to avoid creating any account or uploading any progress
  • Sign out / switch accounts anytime from Settings → Account
  • Delete your account anytime from Settings → Account → Delete account
  • Remove or block a friend from the Friends screen
  • Turn notifications off anytime in your device's settings
  • Restore purchases in the App, and manage/cancel your subscription through the Google Play Store app (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions) or, on iPhone and iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Opt out of personalized advertising — not applicable here, since the App never requests personalized ads for anyone

Data Retention & Deletion

Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists, so we can restore your progress on a new device. You can delete your account at any time in the App: Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile, leaderboard entry, save backup, wallet, friends, records, notification tokens, and the conversations and online games you took part in — conversations and games are removed for both players. For Sign in with Apple accounts we also revoke the App's access with Apple.

Records of completed purchases and redeemed coupon codes are kept after deletion, with your account ID replaced by an irreversible code, so that the same purchase cannot be credited twice. They no longer identify you.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@myappscart.com with the subject "Delete ChessVerse Account" and the email/profile you signed in with. We will process your request within 30 days.
