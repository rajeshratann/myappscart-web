Brain Battle

Privacy Policy

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Brain Battle ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Brain Battle is a single-player brain-training game featuring Word Connect, Number Puzzle, Memory Match, and Color Challenge.

Brain Battle does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information, and does not operate its own servers. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

By installing or using Brain Battle, you agree to this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store personal information. However, the third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain data as described below.

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Game progress (levels completed, stars earned)
  • Coin balance and in-app purchase history
  • Daily challenge status and streak count
  • Achievement badges and unlocks
  • App settings (theme, sound preferences)

This data is stored using the Hive database on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers.

3 Third-Party Services

The following third-party SDKs are integrated into Brain Battle and may collect data independently under their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Brain Battle displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and device information to serve personalised or contextual ads.

  • You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play In-App Purchases

Purchases (e.g., Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment data is handled entirely by Google Play and is subject to Google's Terms of Service. We never receive or store your payment details.

Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play Services for app integrity and update management. Google Play Services may collect anonymous device diagnostics.

4 Advertising ID

The App accesses the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad serving via Google AdMob. This ID is resettable and does not identify you personally. You can opt out or reset it at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
If you purchase the Remove Ads in-app product, ad SDKs will no longer be loaded or called for the remainder of the session and beyond.

5 Children's Privacy

Brain Battle is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Ads displayed by the App are not targeted toward children and are served in accordance with Google AdMob's families policies.

6 Data Retention & Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling Brain Battle permanently removes all locally stored game progress, coins, settings, and purchases from your device.

We do not maintain any server-side user records, so there is nothing to request deletion of on our end.

7 Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Loading advertisements via AdMob
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Daily challenge reminders (optional, you can disable)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restoring scheduled reminders after device restart
AD_ID Advertising ID access for Google AdMob

8 Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. As all data is stored locally on your device, its security is primarily governed by your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

No internet data transmission or external server storage is performed by Brain Battle itself.
