Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Arrow Shooting?

Place one finger in the lower part of the screen to stretch the bow — the further you drag, the more power you charge. Place a second finger anywhere on screen to move the aim crosshair. Release your first finger to fire the arrow toward the crosshair. Watch out for wind — the indicator at the top shows wind direction and strength.

How do I play Knife Throw?

Tap the screen to throw a knife at the spinning target. Make sure your knife doesn't hit one already stuck in the target — timing is everything. Each level adds more pre-stuck knives and increases the spin speed.

Ads are not loading. Is something wrong?

Ad availability depends on your location and network connection. If you are on a slow or restricted network, ads may not load. This is normal and does not indicate a problem with the App. Try again on a different network or check back later.

My high score wasn't saved. Why?

Scores are saved locally on your device using offline storage. Make sure you complete the game round (don't force-close mid-game) so the score has time to save. If your device is very low on storage, local saves may fail — free up some space and try again.

How do I get more coins?

You earn coins by completing game rounds and achieving high scores. You can also watch rewarded video ads in any game's pause or game-over screen to earn bonus coins. Coins are used to continue a game after losing, unlock themes, or get extra arrows/knives.

I used coins to continue but the game still ended. Can I get them back?

Coins spent to continue or revive a game are consumed immediately upon use. We are unable to refund virtual coins. Coins have no monetary value and can be earned freely by playing or watching rewarded ads.

The App crashes or won't open. What should I do?

Try these steps: (1) Force-close and reopen the App. (2) Restart your device. (3) Uninstall and reinstall the App from Google Play. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, Android version, and a description of what happened.

Is GameVault available on iPhone / iOS?

GameVault is currently Android-only. An iOS version may be considered in the future.

How do I delete my data?

All GameVault data is stored locally on your device. Simply uninstall the App from your device to delete all data. We do not store any data on external servers.
