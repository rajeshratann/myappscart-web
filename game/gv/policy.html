GameVault

Privacy Policy

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how GameVault ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. GameVault is a free arcade game collection featuring Arrow Shooting, Knife Throw, Bubble Shooter, Balloon Pop, and Coin Drop.

GameVault does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information, and does not operate its own servers. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

By installing or using GameVault, you agree to this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store personal information. However, third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain data as described below.

Data stored locally on your device only:

  • High scores and game progress per game
  • App settings (sound, music, theme preferences)
  • User profile (optional display name stored locally)

This data is stored using the Hive database on-device only and is never transmitted to any server.

3 Third-Party Services

The following third-party SDKs are integrated and may collect data under their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

GameVault displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and device information to serve personalised or contextual ads.

  • You can opt out of personalised ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps

Google Play Services

Used for app integrity and update management. Google Play Services may collect anonymous device diagnostics. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4 Advertising ID

The App accesses the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) solely for ad serving via Google AdMob. This ID is resettable and does not personally identify you.

  • Reset or opt out: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
Ads are served through Google AdMob's standard ad network. We do not use this ID for any purpose other than ad serving.

5 Children's Privacy

GameVault is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Ads displayed are served in accordance with Google AdMob's families policies.

6 Data Retention & Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling GameVault permanently removes all locally stored scores, settings, and preferences.

We do not maintain any server-side user records, so there is nothing to request deletion of on our end.

8 Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. As all data is stored locally on your device, its security is primarily governed by your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

GameVault itself does not transmit any user data over the internet, nor does it store data on external servers.
