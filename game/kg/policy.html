Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 26, 2026

Overview

Kitty Gaming ("the App") is published by MyAppsCart (operated by Kirosys). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the App, you agree to the terms described here.

Kitty Gaming is a free-to-play entertainment app. All virtual coins are worthless in real money and cannot be exchanged for cash or real-world goods.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data — When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (and, for Google, your profile picture) to create your in-app profile. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email, in which case we receive an Apple relay address instead.
  • Guest play — If you play as a guest, your profile, coins and progress are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers.
  • Display name — A username you choose within the app for leaderboards and game tables.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — Games played, scores, win/loss records, and coin balance, stored to maintain your progress.
  • Device data — Basic device information (such as device model and OS version) may be collected by our ad network, Google AdMob, to deliver ads.
  • Advertising identifiers — On Android, the Google Advertising ID may be used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when the App asks; if you don't, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment or financial information — the App is free and has no in-app purchases
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, or file access

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your game account and profile
  • To save your game progress, coins, and leaderboard position
  • To display your name on public leaderboards (you can change your display name at any time)
  • To show relevant ads via Google AdMob
  • To respond to support requests

Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Sign in with Apple — optional sign-in with your Apple ID (iPhone and iPad)
  • Google Sign-In / Firebase — sign-in, and storing your account, coins and multiplayer game data
  • Google AdMob — advertising and rewarded ads

We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App contains simulated gambling (card games played with virtual coins that have no real-world value) and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the App: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account and its data from our servers immediately; if you signed in with Apple, it also revokes the App's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us at support@myappscart.com with subject "Delete Kitty Gaming Account" and we will process your request within 7 business days.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalised advertising (via your device's ad settings)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@myappscart.com.

Changes to This Policy
