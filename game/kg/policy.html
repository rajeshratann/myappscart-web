Overview

Kitty Gaming ("the App") is published by MyAppsCart (operated by Kirosys). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the App, you agree to the terms described here.

Kitty Gaming is a free-to-play entertainment app. All virtual coins are worthless in real money and cannot be exchanged for cash or real-world goods.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data — When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture to create your in-app profile.
  • Display name — A username you choose within the app for leaderboards and game tables.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — Games played, scores, win/loss records, and coin balance, stored to maintain your progress.
  • Device data — Device model, OS version, and app version, collected for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Advertising identifiers — Google Advertising ID may be collected by our ad network (Google AdMob) to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics data — Anonymised event data (e.g. screen views, feature usage) via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment or financial information — the App is free and has no in-app purchases
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, or file access

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your game account and profile
  • To save your game progress, coins, and leaderboard position
  • To display your name on public leaderboards (you can change your display name at any time)
  • To show relevant ads via Google AdMob
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve the App
  • To respond to support requests

Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase — authentication, analytics, crash reporting
  • Facebook Login — optional authentication via your Facebook account
  • Google AdMob — advertising and rewarded ads

We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, email us at support@myappscart.com with subject "Delete Kitty Gaming Account". We will process your request within 7 business days.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@myappscart.com.
