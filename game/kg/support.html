Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Kitty?

Kitty is a betting card game where players contribute to a central pot (the "kitty"). Each round, players are dealt cards and take turns betting or folding. The player with the strongest hand at the showdown wins the kitty. Use the in-game tutorial for detailed rules.

How do I play Teen Patti?

Teen Patti is a 3-card game similar to poker. You can play "blind" (without looking at your cards) or "seen" (after viewing them). Blind players bet at half the current stake. The game ends in a showdown or when all but one player folds. Highest hand wins the pot.

How do I play Call Break?

Call Break is a trick-taking game where each player bids how many tricks they expect to win. Spades are always trump. You must follow suit if possible; otherwise you can play a spade to win the trick. Meet or exceed your bid each round to score positive points.

How do I play 28?

28 is a Kerala-origin trick-taking game using 32 cards (7 to Ace in all four suits). The goal is to win tricks containing high-value cards (Jack=3, Nine=2, Ace=1, Ten=1). Teams bid to win the round, and the trump suit is revealed progressively. Master the bidding strategy to dominate.

Why do I keep losing virtual coins?

Virtual coins are wagered each time you join a game table. Losing a round means your staked coins go to the winning player. To conserve coins, start at lower-stake tables while learning, collect your daily rewards, and watch rewarded ads for bonus coins.

How do I claim daily rewards?

Open the app and tap the "Daily Reward" banner on the home screen. Log in 7 consecutive days to earn the maximum streak bonus. Missing a day resets your streak to day 1.

Ads are not loading. Is something wrong?

Ad availability depends on your location and network connection. If you are on a slow or restricted network, ads may not load. This is normal — try again on a different network or check back later.

The app crashes or won't open. What should I do?

Try these steps: (1) Force-close and reopen the app. (2) Restart your device. (3) Uninstall and reinstall from Google Play. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, Android version, and a description of what happened.
